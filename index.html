The Work

Resentment doesn't look like anger. It looks like a ceiling on your bank account.

I specialize in helping men and women uncover the resentments and limiting beliefs that are quietly blocking their financial abundance.

The people I work with come to me from every starting point. Some already have real success in their lives but feel capped. Some come to me completely broken, even bankrupt. What they all share is an intuitive sense that a better life is possible for them, they just don't yet know how to get there. It's rarely the economy holding them back. It's an old resentment toward a parent, a partner, or a past version of themselves that never got released. The block was never out there, it's inside, and once you find it, you can release it, and life starts moving differently. I do this work through one-on-one coaching, the Billion Dollar Club (women's and men's), and a self-paced course.

What shifts when the resentment lifts:

  • You see income possibilities you couldn't see before
  • Old limiting beliefs about money and worth lose their grip
  • Improved relationships
  • Clearer goals and direction
  • Increased confidence
  • Better work/life balance
  • Elimination of long-held fears and anxieties
  • Stress reduction
  • Enhanced creativity and performance
  • Improved communication skills

Meet Amanda

I went from five figures to high six figures. Not by working harder. By releasing what was blocking me.

Based in Los Angeles, I have over 15 years of experience as a Transformational Life Coach, and I know this work from the inside because I lived it first.

For most of my life, I battled feelings of inadequacy, self-doubt, perfectionism, and addiction. I finally found the keys to increasing the quality of my life instead of destroying it, and I put down my final addiction in 2007. That's when an incredible journey of self-discovery began, one that continues to evolve today.

My transformation accelerated once I learned to identify and eliminate my own limiting beliefs. Jim Rohn put it simply: "Work harder on yourself than you do on your job." I took that to heart, studying everything I could from teachers like Jim Rohn and Bob Proctor, and applying it to my own life, not just my clients'.

At the time, I was earning a solid five-figure income in my corporate job. Learning to coach others gave me a second income stream, and in just two years, I went from five figures to a high six-figure income. Not because I worked harder. Because I released what was blocking me.

Today, my life is amazing. I wake up every day excited for what's ahead, and it genuinely feels like a dream come true.

My Mission

To help women break through the limiting beliefs, fears, and resentments that prevent them from achieving their goals and dreams, using the tools and techniques I've learned and developed over the years. You don't have to settle for the life you have. You can create the life you want, and I can help you do it.

Life is great: I've been happily married to an incredible man since 1991, I have a beautiful, talented daughter, and an energetic female Jack Russell Terrier who keeps us all on our toes.

For Women

The Billion Dollar Club

"Become a millionaire not for the million dollars, but for what it will make of you to achieve it." Jim Rohn

Guided by life coach Amanda Rae Cooper, the Billion Dollar Club ("BDC") is a group of successful women who are done letting old resentments quietly cap what they earn. Together, we identify the resentment (toward a parent, a partner, a past version of yourself) that's blocking your abundance, and release it, so you can become prosperous in every area of life: career, relationships, health, spirituality, creativity.

This is the right place for you if you want to:

  • Finally name the resentment that's been capping your income
  • Be around positive, fun, successful women while expanding in all areas of your life
  • Improve your relationships
  • Develop new habits and behaviors that support your goals
  • Stop the incessant negative self-talk that occupies way too much space in your mind
  • Move past (self-inflicted) roadblocks by removing your limiting beliefs
  • Learn new tools to calm your fears, doubts, and worries
  • Work less and earn more
  • Thrive

How It Works

  1. Every Thursday, you'll receive the following week's lesson via email from our administrator, Katherine Pepe.
  2. Briefly review the lesson (email + attachment).
  3. During our Sunday Zoom session, Amanda will explain the lesson.
  4. During the week, work on the lesson. This may include watching a video, listening to a recording, writing out your goals, and more.

What if I miss a Sunday?

No worries. Sessions are recorded and posted in the group's WhatsApp chat immediately after each one, just listen to the audio when you have a chance.

What if I get behind?

This is personal development, not a class to ace. If you try to get an "A," you'll burn yourself out. The people who get the most out of this are the ones willing to half-ass it and keep moving. If you fall behind, just pick up with the most current lesson rather than going back; it's all repetition, and you'll eventually hear everything you need.
