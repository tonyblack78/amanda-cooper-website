The Work

Don't just dream about it. Let me help you do it.

Transformational life coaching helps individuals become self-aware, self-motivated, and self-directed.

It's a powerful way to help people realize their full potential and live their best lives. I offer a range of coaching services — one-on-one coaching, group coaching, and workshops, in person or via Zoom.

Benefits include:

  • Improved relationships
  • Better decision-making
  • Clearer goals and direction
  • Increased confidence
  • Better work/life balance
  • Overcoming limiting beliefs
  • Elimination of long-held fears and anxieties
  • Stress reduction
  • Enhanced creativity and performance
  • Improved communication skills

Meet Amanda

Live your best life ever. If I can do it, so can you.

Based in Los Angeles, I have over 15 years of experience as a Transformational Life Coach and have helped and empowered hundreds of men and women transform their lives and achieve their goals.

Using a variety of tools and techniques, I help clients reframe their life experiences to remove longstanding blocks and make profound shifts in their beliefs and behaviors — achieving significant, lasting change in a relatively short period of time.

Battling feelings of inadequacy, self-doubt, perfectionism, and addiction throughout my life, I finally found the keys to increasing the quality of my life instead of destroying it. I put down my final addiction in 2007 and began an incredible journey of self-discovery that continues to evolve. My transformation occurred rapidly once I developed the skills to change my thoughts — and today, every day is a dream come true.

My Mission

To devote as much time as possible to giving back and helping men and women achieve their goals and dreams with the tools and techniques I've learned and developed over the years. It's time for everyone to experience their best lives ever.

The Women's Group

The Billion Dollar Club

"Become a millionaire not for the million dollars, but for what it will make of you to achieve it." Jim Rohn

Guided by life coach Amanda Rae Cooper, the Billion Dollar Club ("BDC") is a group of like-minded women dedicated to becoming prosperous in all areas of their lives — career, relationships, health, spirituality, creativity — through personal development.

This is the right place for you if you want to:

  • Be around positive, fun, successful women while expanding in all areas of your life
  • Improve your relationships
  • Develop new habits and behaviors that support your goals
  • Stop the incessant negative self-talk that occupies way too much space in your mind
  • Move past (self-inflicted) roadblocks by removing your limiting beliefs
  • Learn new tools to calm your fears, doubts, and worries
  • Work less and earn more
  • Thrive

How It Works

  1. Every Thursday, you'll receive the following week's lesson via email from our administrator, Katherine Pepe.
  2. Briefly review the lesson (email + attachment).
  3. During our Sunday Zoom session, Amanda will explain the lesson.
  4. During the week, work on the lesson — this may include watching a video, listening to a recording, writing out your goals, and more.

What if I miss a Sunday?

No worries. Sessions are recorded and posted in the group's WhatsApp chat immediately after each one — just listen to the audio when you have a chance.

What if I get behind?

This is personal development, not a class to ace. If you try to get an "A," you'll burn yourself out — the people who get the most out of this are the ones willing to half-ass it and keep moving. If you fall behind, just pick up with the most current lesson rather than going back; it's all repetition, and you'll eventually hear everything you need.
