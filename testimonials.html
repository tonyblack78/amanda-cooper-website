Real people. Real shifts.

Amanda has coached hundreds of women and men through the Billion Dollar Club, 1:1 sessions, and the course. Here's a small sample of what they've shared about the work.

Shelton W.

Brentwood, Los Angeles

Shelton began working with Amanda as a mentor and later became a full client. Before, his marriage was struggling, his career had no momentum, and complaining had become a habit. Amanda's blunt, non-blaming approach brought fast results, helping him release old resentments and limiting beliefs. Ten years later, his marriage is strong, and he's a top real estate agent in Los Angeles leading a team of ten.

Jordan M.

Venice Beach, California

Jordan has worked with Amanda for eight years. In that time, he went from broke to owning several profitable businesses, from single to married with a son, and from constant self-criticism to self-acceptance. Santa Barbara, California

Katherine was reluctant to hire a coach but was persuaded by a mutual friend. After going through Amanda's program, the pain is gone, her anger and stress have eased, her income has grown beyond what she expected, and dating advice from Amanda helped her find a happy marriage.

She credits Amanda's guidance for the turnaround.
